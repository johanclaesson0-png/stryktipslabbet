# V29 research
Independent experiment: strict imported retrospective result claims, same-match frozen market odds, validated form overlay. No live model or stake modification. Individual skill = hit minus market probability of the picked outcome; shrink toward zero, by identity, league, sign and market probability bucket. Publisher groups share influence. Chronological test by coupon: prior results must be observed before next cutoff, weights are chosen only from preceding prediction errors. Minimum 50 prior matched positions and 8 rounds to start selecting weights, 500 holdout positions and 30 rounds before even considering promotion; independent prospective holdout and manual review always required. Community and player effects are blocked absent verified temporally matched input. Current external odds converter is offline only: verified fixture mapping, licensed JSON snapshot strictly before common cutoff and kickoff, two complete bookmakers, no-vig consensus. Data source options: https://the-odds-api.com/historical-odds-data/ (paid historical access, separate permission/license); https://football-data.co.uk/data (private use; not automated AI training). Do not buy, fetch with unauthorized credentials, or use closing odds as preclose odds. No external snapshot is integrated until supplied and reviewed. Current pilot uses original frozen market snapshots only.
